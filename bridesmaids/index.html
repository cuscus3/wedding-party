Welcome to Milwaukee 🤍

The day before

Friday is low-key. Everything here is optional, so come to what you can and enjoy the city.

❤
  1. 12:00 PM
    Wedding party lunch
    Optional! If you're already in Milwaukee, come join Tanya, Ryan, and the wedding party for light snacks and drinks at Lakefront Brewery. If you're still traveling or have other plans, absolutely no worries.
    Lakefront Brewery ↗
  2. 3:00 PM
    Ceremony rehearsal
    Also optional. We'll do a quick 30 to 45 minute run-through so everyone can see the space. If you can't make it, don't stress. During the ceremony the bridesmaids and groomsmen alternate walking down the aisle and then take their seats, so it's very easy to pick up on wedding day.
    Villa Terrace ↗

Wedding Day 🥂

The full run of show

The whole day, from morning in the bridal suite to the after-party. The big one: be at Villa Terrace by 8:00 AM.

❤
  1. 8:00 AM
    Bridesmaids arrive
    Please plan to be at Villa Terrace (Bridal Suite) by 8:00 so we can settle in and start hair and makeup right away. We'll spend the morning together getting ready, eating, drinking mimosas, taking photos, and getting excited!
    2220 N Terrace Ave ↗
  2. 12:00 PM
    Cameras arrive
    Photography, videography, and our content creator start arriving. We'll take getting-ready photos and video together, including some cute shots in our getting-ready looks. Tanya is providing matching pajamas and slippers for everyone! Heads up: the museum is open to the public until 2:00, so there may be guests around parts of the property.
  3. 1:00 PM
    Final getting ready
    Hair and makeup should be wrapping up. Tanya gets into her dress (Hala to zip her in), and bridesmaids start getting fully dressed. Goal: everyone completely dressed and photo-ready by 2:00.
  4. 1:30 PM
    First look 🤍
    Tanya and Ryan have their first look and couple portraits. Bridesmaids can finish last-minute touch-ups and be ready for wedding-party photos.
  5. 2:15 PM
    Wedding party photos
    Everyone fully dressed and photo-ready! We'll head from Villa Terrace down to the gardens (we'll likely walk the stairs together, totally manageable), then the shuttle brings us back up before guests arrive.
  6. 3:15 PM
    Photos wrap + freshen up
    Hard stop around 3:15. Head back inside to freshen up, touch up hair and makeup, and grab water. Guests start arriving around 3:30, so we'll stay tucked away from guest areas.
  7. 3:30 PM
    Ceremony time 🌿
    The bridal party takes the last shuttle down to the gardens before the ceremony. There's a Prosecco cart in the gardens, so enjoy a welcome drink after the ceremony.
  8. 4:00 PM
    Ceremony begins
    Bridesmaids and groomsmen alternate walking down the aisle, then take their seats for the ceremony.
  9. 4:30 PM
    Cocktail hour 🥂
    Needed for family photos? Stay down in the gardens after the ceremony. Not needed? Grab a Prosecco and take the shuttle up to the courtyard for cocktail hour with appetizers, drinks, and live music. Tanya and Ryan join after family photos.
  10. 5:45 PM
    Dinner seating
    Guests are called to their seats. The bar closes temporarily from 5:45 to 6:30 while catering transitions into dinner, so grab anything you want beforehand.
    Bar closed 5:45–6:30
  11. 6:00 PM
    Reception begins
    Tanya and Ryan's introduction, then cake cutting, first dance, and welcome speeches, into dinner (salad first, then dinner plates). Dessert and coffee go out by 7:45 on the credenza in the guest hall near the terrace. Grab some once it's out, and let others know!
  12. 8:15 PM
    Let's party 💃
    Tanya and Ryan make their second entrance with Lebanese drummers and saxophone, followed by Lebanese dancing and then open dancing.
  13. 9:00 PM
    Late-night pizza 🍕
  14. 10:15 PM
    Last call + last song
  15. 10:30 PM
    Reception ends
    Official end of the reception. Then up to the rooftop for the after-party!
  16. After 10:30 PM
    After-party 🌙
    Not ready for the night to end? We're keeping it going on the 7Seventy7 rooftop with beer and drinks on us, so just come on up! Uber/Lyft is easiest, and street parking is around too, so plan accordingly if you'll be drinking.
    7Seventy7 Rooftop ↗
